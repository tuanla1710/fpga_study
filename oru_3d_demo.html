Board 120 × 80 mm. The RJ45 MGMT port, which also carries PoE, is on the left edge next to the debug cluster. Antennas are on the right edge. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.

Hover a part to see its name.

The Agilex 5 and the AFE7950 sit close together so the 8.11 Gb/s JESD lanes stay short. The SI5518 sits between those two chips. The 25 GbE quad is on the SFP side and does not share a refclk with JESD.
